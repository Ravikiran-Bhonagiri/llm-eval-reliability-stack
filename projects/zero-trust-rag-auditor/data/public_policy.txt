Policy Title: General Employee Guidelines
Date: 2024-01-01

1. Holiday Calendar
- New Year's Day: Closed
- Independence Day: Closed
- Thanksgiving: Closed
- Christmas: Closed

2. Dress Code
- Business Casual is the standard.
- Jeans are allowed on Fridays.
- No sandals or shorts.

3. Remote Work
- Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.
- Core hours are 10am to 3pm.
